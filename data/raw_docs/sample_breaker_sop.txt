This sample SOP covers routine maintenance of HV circuit breakers. It is SAMPLE
content for demonstration only.

1. Scope

This SOP applies to SF6 and vacuum circuit breakers from 11 kV to 220 kV.

2. Contact Resistance Measurement

Contact resistance is measured with a 100 A digital low-resistance ohmmeter.
Isolate the breaker, earth both sides and obtain a permit. Measure each pole
in the closed position. A typical acceptance limit is 100 micro-ohms or the
OEM value, whichever is lower. Pole-to-pole variation greater than 50 percent
indicates a problem contact.

3. Timing Test

Use a breaker analyser to record close, open and close-open operations.
Pole dispersion should not exceed 2 ms. Compare velocity and travel curves
with the OEM signature. Increased operating time suggests mechanism wear,
sticking latches or low operating pressure.

4. SF6 Gas Checks

Measure moisture content with a dew-point analyser corrected to 20 degrees
Celsius. A typical limit is 150 ppmv for breakers. Check density monitor
settings and leak history. Do not vent SF6 to atmosphere; use gas handling
carts with filtration.

5. Operating Mechanism

Inspect the mechanism for oil leaks (hydraulic) or pressure loss (pneumatic).
Check auxiliary switches, anti-pumping function, and trip/close coil currents.
Lubricate per the OEM schedule.

6. Safety

Switching operations for testing require the breaker isolated from the network
with clear visible breaks. Earth both sides. Remote operation only, with
personnel clear of the mechanism.
