This sample SOP covers routine maintenance of oil-filled power transformers in
transmission substations. It is SAMPLE content for demonstration only.

1. Scope

This SOP applies to 33 kV–220 kV oil-filled power transformers with on-load tap
changers and Buchholz protection.

2. Monthly Checks

- Check oil level in conservator and OLTC diverter.
- Check silica-gel breather colour; replace crystals when two-thirds pink.
- Record load, top-oil and winding temperatures.
- Inspect for oil leaks around gaskets, radiators and valves.

3. Insulation Resistance Testing

The insulation resistance test is performed with a 5 kV megger. Isolate the
transformer, apply working earths, and obtain a permit-to-work. Measure IR
between HV and earth, LV and earth, and HV and LV for one minute. The
polarisation index is the ratio of the 10-minute to the 1-minute reading.
A polarisation index below 1.5 indicates moisture. Values must be corrected
to 20 degrees Celsius for trending.

4. Oil Sampling and DGA

Draw oil from the bottom sample valve using a gas-tight syringe. Label the
sample with date, time, load and top-oil temperature. Dissolved gas analysis
detects incipient faults. Hydrogen above 100 ppm requires attention. Any
acetylene in the main tank indicates arcing and must be investigated
immediately. Interpret results using IEC 60599 ratio methods.

5. Buchholz Relay Inspection

Check the float and alarm circuit operation quarterly. Gas collected in the
chamber should be analysed; combustible gas indicates an internal fault.
Do not reset the alarm without investigating the cause.

6. Safety

All work requires a permit-to-work. Isolate, earth and verify dead before
touching any terminal. Wear appropriate PPE including arc-rated clothing.
Follow site switching procedures.
