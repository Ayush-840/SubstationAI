# Product Requirement Document (PRD)
## Substation Maintenance AI Chatbot - Retrieval & Safety Overhaul

### 1. Executive Summary & Problem Overview
The Substation Maintenance Intelligent Chatbot is designed to assist field engineers and technical personnel with fast, accurate, and safety-compliant answers regarding maintenance procedures, testing limits, safety guidelines, and troubleshooting across various equipment classes (Transformers, Circuit Breakers, Reactors, Instrument Transformers, Surge Arresters).

Testing and deficiency logs identified critical operational failures:
* **Topic Collapse**: Non-transformer queries (e.g., Circuit Breaker contact resistance) repeatedly retrieved Transformer Insulation Resistance (IR) / Polarization Index (PI) content.
* **Hallucinated Universal Limits**: Context-specific test parameters were returned as universal limits for all equipment.
* **Uncalibrated Confidence Scores**: The system reported "High Confidence" even when providing irrelevant context.
* **Broken Source Metadata**: Citations frequently displayed incomplete information like `Page: None`.

This PRD outlines the functional and technical capabilities required to fix these core vulnerabilities.

---

### 2. Goals & Success Metrics
* **Query Intent & Equipment Classification Accuracy**: $\ge 98\%$ accuracy in identifying target equipment and test types.
* **Context Isolation**: $0\%$ leakage of Transformer IR context into queries for other equipment classes.
* **Citation Traceability**: $100\%$ valid, traceable citations containing Document Name, Version, Section, and Page Number (eliminating `Page: None` and `Page: -`)[cite: 1].
* **Safety & Limit Validation**: $0\%$ single-value answers for parameters that depend on voltage class, temperature, or OEM specifications[cite: 1].
* **Calibrated Confidence Scoring**: Alignment between reported confidence scores and actual retrieval/semantic relevance[cite: 1].

---

### 3. Target User Personas
1. **Substation Maintenance Engineer**: Needs precise step-by-step test procedures, standards, and equipment parameter checks during active maintenance.
2. **Field Technician**: Prefers symptom-driven troubleshooting queries (e.g., "unusually low insulation reading") and safety limit checks[cite: 1].
3. **Safety & Compliance Auditor**: Requires traceable references to industrial standards (IEEE/IEC/IS) and operational manuals[cite: 1].

---

### 4. Functional Requirements

#### FR-1: Natural Language Query Understanding & Routing
* The system shall extract the following entities from user queries[cite: 1]:
  * `Equipment Class` (Transformer, Circuit Breaker, Reactor, etc.)[cite: 1]
  * `Test Type` (IR/PI, Contact Resistance, DGA, Tan Delta, etc.)[cite: 1]
  * `Symptom/Issue` (e.g., high contact resistance, low insulation reading)[cite: 1]
  * `Negative Constraints` (e.g., "Do not include Transformer IR")[cite: 1]
* The system shall parse ambiguous, natural-language symptom descriptions into structured maintenance search parameters without requiring exact technical keywords[cite: 1].

#### FR-2: Domain-Isolated Semantic Retrieval
* The system shall execute metadata-filtered hybrid search (Dense Embeddings + Sparse BM25) to isolate relevant knowledge domains[cite: 1].
* When a specific equipment class is identified, the system shall apply a hard pre-filter restricting context retrieval strictly to that equipment category[cite: 1].
* The system shall clear session-based retrieval buffers between queries to prevent persistent default context leakage[cite: 1].

#### FR-3: Applicability Validation & Conflict Resolution
* The system shall check whether retrieved threshold values are dependent on specific conditions (e.g., voltage rating, temperature correction to $20^{\circ}\text{C}$, OEM specs)[cite: 1].
* The system shall reject providing a single "universal" acceptance limit when parameters vary by equipment class or condition[cite: 1].
* If retrieved sources provide conflicting values (e.g., $500\,\text{M}\Omega$ vs $1000\,\text{M}\Omega$), the chatbot shall highlight the conflict, present both values, and list the conditions for each[cite: 1].

#### FR-4: Strict Grounded Generation & Full Citations
* Generated answers must be strictly grounded in the retrieved chunks[cite: 1].
* Every technical limit or step must include inline citations formatted as:  
  `[Document Name | Section X.Y | Page Z]`[cite: 1].
* Null or missing metadata (`Page: None`) must be caught and prevented at the ingestion phase[cite: 1].

#### FR-5: Calibrated Multi-Factor Confidence Engine
* Confidence shall be calculated using a weighted combination of Intent Match Score, Vector Similarity Score, and Cross-Encoder Reranker Score[cite: 1].
* If confidence falls below the minimum threshold ($0.55$), the chatbot shall request clarification or state that relevant information was not found instead of generating an unverified answer[cite: 1].

---

### 5. Non-Functional Requirements
* **Response Latency**: End-to-end response time under $3.5$ seconds for complex multi-chunk queries.
* **Accuracy & Safety**: High-precision output adhering to industrial standards (IEEE, IEC, IS) and safety guidelines[cite: 1].
* **Traceability & Audit Logging**: Full diagnostic logging of query transformations, intent classification, retrieved chunks, rerank scores, and final prompts[cite: 1].
